01Project overview

Rapid, sub-millimeter positioning for target tracking

Maritime SONAR systems demand absolute spatial accuracy with zero operational lag. This project focused on designing a multi-axis platform capable of interpreting real-time coordinate data and mechanically driving the SONAR to its exact 3D orientation under strict time constraints.

!

Legacy Version

Original setup relied on manual overhead cranes, suffered from a 6-inch vertical error margin, and operated entirely without fail-safe mechanisms.

✓

Automated Upgrade

Manual rigging replaced with HMI-controlled motors and winches, accuracy to 1mm while integrating strict hardware limit switches and master-slave safety controls.

02My Contribution

From operational requirements to validated physical feasibility.

01

Requirements & Feasibility Analysis

Examined the legacy system's shortcomings and define achievable precision targets, aligned the core design goals with available hardware resources and component accuracy limits.

02

CAD Development & Design Release

Translated concepts into detailed SolidWorks CAD models and validated structural integrity through FEA. Generated comprehensive engineering drawings and coordinated with manufacturers to verify production feasibility.

03

Simulation, Tolerancing & Fabrication

Executed simulations to ensure all structural requirements were met under marine conditions, managed component fabrication, specifying strict GD&T tolerances to guarantee a seamless assembly.

04

HMI & Systems Testing

Executed the complete physical build and installed the control architecture. Developed the custom LabVIEW HMI and conducted testing to verify the 1mm translational accuracy, systematically troubleshooting hardware integration.

04

Finalization & Documentation

Delivered comprehensive system documentation and instruction manuals and applied anti-corrosion surface treatments to protect the assembly in saline environments.

03Technical approach

From manual rigging to automated tracking.

Assess

Audit the legacy platform setup, identifying shortfalls and hazards, defining targets and constraints.

Engineer

Develop the multi-axis CAD architecture in SolidWorks, defining GD&T and validating load paths via FEA.

Integrate

Bridge the physical build with the control architecture, installing motorized winches and the LabVIEW HMI.

Validate

Commission the final hardware, testing system accuracy and verifying all fail-safes.

Why sub-millimeter tracking?High-fidelity marine target acquisition demands absolute spatial accuracy. A fractional degree of mechanical lag or a few millimeters of displacement at the source magnifies into massive target acquisition errors, rendering the sensor data useless.

05Results

An industry-ready prototype validated for targeted marine environments.

The project delivered a functional, multi-axis positioning platform capable of executing spatial coordinates with target precision. By successfully integrating robust structural hardware with HMI, the prototype established a highly reliable, field-ready foundation for high-fidelity maritime tracking applications.
