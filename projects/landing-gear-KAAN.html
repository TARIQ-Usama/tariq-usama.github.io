01Project overview

Optimizing structural performance within defined assembly envelopes.

My work sat at the intersection of structural performance, manufacturability, and mass reduction targets. Using baseline reference models, the objective was to apply topology optimization to achieve feasible mass and stress targets, ensuring the final CAD model remained viable for the facility.

02My Contribution

Component redesign backed by topology analysis and clear manufacturing proposals.

01

Requirements & Constraint Mapping

Evaluated existing landing gear models and assembly constraints, establishing strict maximum stress thresholds and mass reduction targets based on the provided details and aerospace material catalogue.

02

CAD Remodeling & Topology Setup

Collaborated closely with my supervisor to identify specific components for re-engineering, locally recreating baseline CAD models in Siemens NX to prepare the design space for topology optimization.

03

Verification & CAM Simulation

Validated the optimized geometries against our set targets through continuous supervisory review, developed CAM simulations to model and evaluate viable manufacturing pathways.

04

Engineering Proposals & Documentation

Delivered final CAD models and detailed engineering drawings with assembly instructions, provided technical presentation on proposed production pathway backed by the simulated CAM files.

03Engineering workflow

Closing the loop between definition, optimization and verification.

Define Boundaries

Translate localized installation space and performance targets into strict, finalized engineering goals.

Optimize Geometry

Drive mass reduction for selected components through iterative topology optimization in Siemens NX.

Verify & Review

Validate the optimized structural response against stress limits and refine the geometry through continuous supervisor feedback.

Document & Proposal

Lock the final CAD models, generate detailed technical drawings, and deliver a CAM-validated production proposal.
