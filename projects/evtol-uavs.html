01Project overview

Fixed-wing endurance engineered for critical surveillance and payload delivery.

The primary challenge was finding the perfect convergence of airfoil selection, precise fuselage sizing, aerodynamic simulation, and lightweight manufacturing to create a mission-ready aircraft for both intruder detection and emergency supply drops.

TKF

Intruder Detection Mission

Maximizing range, endurance and platform stability to support optical payloads for sustained area surveillance.

DFC

Emergency Supply Mission

Optimize lift-to-drag characteristics and structural integrity to immediately and accurately deploy emergency payloads in affected areas.

02My Contribution

Mechanical design with system integration kept in view.

01

Mission Profiling & Conceptual Sizing

Defined critical drone characteristics by translating mission needs into actionable engineering targets, utilizing MS Excel to establish baseline parameters for Max take-off weight, range, and cruise speed.

02

Aerodynamic Analysis & Validation

Evaluated multiple airfoil geometries to achieve optimal flight parameters and stability characteristics, validated for complete aircraft and selected airfoil through 2D simulations using XFLR-5.

03

CAD Modeling & Material Selection

Designed the wings and fuselage architecture in SolidWorks and revalidated through 3D analysis in Ansys Fluent. Helped in selecting lightweight, accessible materials to balance structural integrity with strict competition weight limits.

04

Hardware Integration & Assembly

Constructed the physical airframe and supported final assembly. Collaborated closely with avionics sub-teams to ensure precise structural packaging of equipment within the fuselage.

03Development process

A complete aircraft loop—from requirements to flight.

Define

Translate mission needs into configuration, performance, payload and autonomy requirements.

Design & Analyse

Iterate aerodynamic layout, propulsion, CAD geometry and structural response.

Manufacture

Produce components from selected materials, drawings, tooling and assembled airframe hardware.

Integrate & Fly

Install propulsion and avionics, configure flight software and progress to prototype flight.

System-level lessonFor a UAV, a local improvement can move mass, centre of gravity, power demand or control authority elsewhere. Design decisions were evaluated as aircraft-level trades rather than isolated component choices.
